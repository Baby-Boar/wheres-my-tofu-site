Don't spill the water

水

There's a glass of water riding along with the tofu. Get it down the mountain still full and the customer will never know how you drove.

  • Smooth is fast

    Clean lines and tidy drifts keep the water in. Yes, you can drift. Just do it nicely.

  • Mistakes will cost you

    Snap the wheel, stab the brakes, see-saw out of a slide... and there goes the water. The tofu won't be far behind.
